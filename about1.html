About Dr. Puneet Kumar

A highly accomplished physician with over 12 years of experience in Internal Medicine, Diabetes, Hypertension, Thyroid Disorders, Emergency Care, and Preventive Health.

Profile Overview

Dr. Puneet Kumar is a seasoned Internal Medicine specialist with extensive experience in managing acute and chronic medical conditions. He has a special interest in Diabetes Mellitus and its complications, supported by multiple fellowships and certifications from prestigious medical institutions worldwide.

His evidence-based approach, combined with a patient-first attitude, has earned him a reputation for delivering high-quality, compassionate care with a focus on long-term health outcomes.
